Next Year's Square

Last year's seeds, this year's garden.

The garden book is finished, but a tin is still on the gate. Open it, and last year's sunflower seeds are there — twelve of them, kept from the harvest. Tuck a few into a new square by the gate, and see what a second year does. Nothing here can wilt, and nothing is lost: a cleared tin is just a tin waiting to be filled again.
