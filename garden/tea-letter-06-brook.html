From the brook stones themselves,
written by water

For El Phantea — the paper dried crinkled, like all water-letters do.

One pressed leaf, glued at the corner, still smelling faintly of rain.

“We keep the middle one warm for small feet; tell Pipkin the green moss is his step now.”

— The Brook Stones

Tap the water to ripple it. Then tap the stones, one by one, and feel which one the brook kept warm.

🍃 Unlocks in the book: the hop-home page's stepping-stone illustration changes — the moss on Pipkin's stone a brighter green, forever, on every reread. Nothing is awarded. The world simply answers.
