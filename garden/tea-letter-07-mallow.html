El Phantea's Tea Letters · № 7 — a reward letter
✉️

This letter is still on its way

It arrives for readers who have sat a little while under the willow. Open Mallow’s Little While first, and the door stays open afterwards.

🌿 🌿 🌿
the chipped one — string tied

From Mallow, under the willow

For El Phantea — written slowly, in pencil, on dusk-grey paper.

One pear, cut into thin moons — pressed flat at the corner, still sweet.

“El Phantea, the kettle is on the flat pebble and the moth has settled on the lid again. I set out the six cups and tied a length of string around the one with the small chip so it would not be forgotten.”

“The reader sat down while I thought, and the evening had room for both of us. For whenever you have a little while.”

— Mallow

Tap the moth on the kettle — gently. It has settled for the evening.

🫚 Unlocks in the book: the keepsake reverse of the hidden chapter — the seventh cup, already waiting there, now carries a faint margin note in pebble-beige: “Mallow left this one for you.”
№ 7 · a reward letter — it arrives after Mallow is found, and this page veils itself until then. The bundle of six stands complete; this one waits under the willow.
