El Phantea's Tea Letters · № 8 — the postmistress writes back

From El Phantea, at the tea table

For the reader who keeps the letters — written in a careful hand, on ivory paper.

A miniature trunk, carried everywhere — the letters travel in it, and so does the tea.

“Darling, the dusk-rose blend has travelled safely in my trunk with every letter you opened. I pour for the smallest cup first, always, so the steam rises kindly for all.”

“One lump, or two? Tea makes everything better — and a second lump never hurt a friend.”

— El Phantea

Tap the sugar bowl — one lump, or two?
