From Wee, by hoofprint,
with one sock wet

For El Phantea — hello from the wet side of the brook.

🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾 🐾

Six neat trot marks — and one smudged extra, for excitement.

“I hopped the brook stones twice today and only got one sock wet; the stones said tell the elephant hello from their wet side.”

— Wee

Tap the stones, in order, and hop Wee across.

🎵 One stone, two stone, three stone, four —
mind the sock and mind the door.
🫖 Unlocks in the book: a short skipping rhyme the narration hums later, whenever the stones are crossed — and the wet sock is remembered fondly, never scolded.
