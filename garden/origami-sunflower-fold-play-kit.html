Fold a Sunflower

Four folds, one season. Tuck her in, water her, breathe once slow — then three gentle taps, and she opens. Finish all four, then unfold it and read the season in the creases.

Pipkin: A flat square of watercolour paper — and a seed to tuck in.

A flat square of watercolour paper. Name her, then tuck her in.

How it plays. Every fold is failure-free: a tap out of order or a double tap only rustles softly and nothing moves; tapping a fold jumps straight to it — no fold can be broken, only peeked under. The bloom is three gentle taps, the way the book opens her, and Pipkin only ever says his own lines. When she is open, unfolding lays the sheet flat again, but every crease stays behind in gold: a map of the season. From the Origami play seed in the garden's design notes; paper, peach underside and one red thread, in the Storeybrook Origami manner.
