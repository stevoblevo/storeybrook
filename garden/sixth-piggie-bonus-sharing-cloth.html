The cloth, hung out after the sharing.

After the feast was shared, someone hung the tablecloth on the line to air — and stitched a small picture into its corner while it dried. Not in the middle of the picture, where everyone looks. At the edge, where only someone tidying up would notice.

A bonus, not a sixth clue: the five clues already open Mallow's door. This one is only for readers who linger after the sharing.
