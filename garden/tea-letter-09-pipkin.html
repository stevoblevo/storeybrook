El Phantea's Tea Letters · № 9 — Pipkin writes
❤ ❤ ❤

Eight small footprints, hopping — and one pressed sprout, kept.

From Pipkin, in footprints

For the reader who has been hopping alongside — written in eight small footprints and one pressed sprout.

“I grew again, did you see?
Wee!”

Tap the pressed sprout — Pipkin hops.

🌱 Unlocks in the book: on a later visit to the sequel’s garden square, the sprout beside the sunflower carries one very faint pink bud that was not there on first reading — only a margin detail, no text, no new scene.
№ 9 · Pipkin writes — open to every reader, since he hops beside the reader from the first page. He writes in footprints; the two lines are his own words, all four of them kept whole across the card. The bud stays closed — the bloom is the sequel’s, not this letter’s.
