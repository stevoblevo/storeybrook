Fold a Piggie

Four folds, one piggie. Tap the sheet — or a fold below — and the paper folds itself. Finish all four, then unfold it and read the journey in the creases.

A flat square of watercolour paper. Begin with the ear.

How it plays. Every fold is failure-free: a wild drag simply springs back, and tapping a fold jumps straight to it — no fold can be broken, only peeked under. When the piggie is complete, unfolding lays the sheet flat again, but every crease stays behind in gold: a map of the journey home. From the Origami play seed in the garden's design notes; paper, peach underside and one red thread, in the Storeybrook Origami manner.
