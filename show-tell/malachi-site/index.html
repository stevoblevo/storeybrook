Editing and caption uncertainties

The full recording is 38:07.272. This review provides ten separate excerpts; the continuous edited cut is kept private. Captions are machine transcribed and locally reviewed, not verified word for word by Malachi. “In line of that” may be “in light of that”; a transition near the original 34:33 remains unclear. The gospel passage retains Christ’s voluntary death, God’s wrath in our place and resurrection. The closing retains the caveat that suffering is not always personal folly.
