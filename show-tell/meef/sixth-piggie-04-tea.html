The same blue as the feast napkin, the thread on the well's lowest stone, and the thread in Hazel's unfinished pear. Four clues, one colour, one small guest.

Lift the tablecloth hem first — the little chair is behind it, the way small things are. Then draw it out.

🪑 The sixth chair slides out into the late light, and there it is: a tiny cup, already poured and still warm, as if someone small had only just stepped away to smell the roses. She was here for the tea, too — the chair remembers, even when the counting forgets. Nobody names her. That is the clue.
