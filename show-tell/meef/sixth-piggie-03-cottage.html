The Sixth Piggie · A clue at Hazel's cottage

Behind the mending basket, a hollow.

Hazel's window seat is the quietest place in the book. The kettle sings, the curtains stir, and nobody sits down to count who has been sitting there. Move the basket, and settle the cushion the way a small guest would have left it.

The window seat

The same blue as the feast napkin, and the thread on the well's lowest stone. Unfinished — like someone meant to come back to it.

Set the mending basket aside first — the cushion is behind it, the way small things are. Then touch the cushion.

🛋️ The cushion keeps one shallow hollow, as if someone small sat there a little while and then slipped out to the garden. The curtain lifts, and outside — just for a moment — a little ear-shaped shadow passes the window. Nobody names her. That is the clue.
