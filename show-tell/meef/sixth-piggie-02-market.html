The Sixth Piggie · A clue at the market

Beside the well, beneath the apron.

The market is busy, the narration is shopping, and nobody is counting. But the damp patch by the well keeps a small, damp record. Lift the apron, and follow the prints the way the pictures do.

🐾 Six prints crossed the damp patch. Five went to market. One turned toward the thyme — and Pipkin, mid-shopping, glanced after it. Nobody counted. That is the clue.
